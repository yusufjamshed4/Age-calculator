How It Works

Our free Age Calculator uses precise date arithmetic that correctly handles leap years, month lengths, and edge cases (such as February 29 birthdays). Simply enter your date of birth — optionally choose any past or future reference date — and instantly see:

  • Exact age in years, months and days
  • Total months, weeks and days lived
  • Next birthday date and countdown in days

All calculations run entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
